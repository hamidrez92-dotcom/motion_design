    Plugins are bundles of skills and connectors packaged for one job. Instead of hunting for skills one by one, you click once and Claude gets a whole playbook for marketing, finance, legal, design or running your day. This guide shows you how to install the five I use, the one prompt that sets them all up for you, and the best first thing to try with each.

    What you get: Install all 5 in about two minutes, Marketing, Finance, Legal, Design and Productivity, one setup prompt that teaches Claude about you, the best first prompt for each plugin, fixes if a plugin doesn't show up, and 6 copyable prompts.

    The 5 Plugins

    The plan · 3 steps

    Before you start

    Your account

    Any paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise). Plugins aren't on the Free plan.

    Your materials

    Nothing yet. A contract, a design or a spreadsheet helps later.

    Your first goal

    All 5 installed and one real piece of work done with each.

    All five are made by Anthropic and free to add on any paid plan.

    PluginWhat it does for you
    MarketingCampaign plans, social posts, newsletters, email sequences, SEO audits and competitor briefs, all in your brand voice.
    FinanceIncome statements, variance breakdowns, reconciliations and month-end close work from your numbers.
    LegalContract review, NDA triage, compliance checks and risk flags before you sign anything.

    Maverick's tip

    Plugins work in Claude chat on the web, the Claude desktop app and Cowork. Anything you add is saved to your account, so it follows you everywhere you sign in.

    1

    Install All 5

    Do this on a computer, at claude.ai or in the Claude desktop app.

    1. Open Customize. Click Customize in the left sidebar.
    2. Open Plugins. Click the Plugins tab at the top, then Discover.
    3. Add each one. Search Marketing and click the + on the card made by Anthropic. Do the same for Finance, Legal, Design and Productivity.
    4. Check they're in. Click Yours. All five should be listed there.

    Maverick's tip

    Not sure a plugin is worth it? Click Try on the card to test it in a chat first.

    Heads up: Search shows lots of plugins with similar names. Pick the ones that say by Anthropic under the name.

    The Setup Prompt

    Open a new chat and send this. Claude asks you a few quick questions and sets all five plugins up around your work, so everything after this sounds like you.

    Prompt 1 · Copy & paste
    I just installed your Marketing, Finance, Legal, Design and Productivity plugins. Set them up for me.

Ask me one question at a time, wait for my answer, then ask the next. Find out:
1. What I do and who I do it for.
2. My brand voice, with an example of something I've written.
3. The numbers I track for money (revenue, costs, anything else).
4. The kinds of contracts I sign most.
5. What I'm designing or building right now.
6. My top 3 priorities this week.

When you're done, give me a short summary of what you learned, then tell me the single best first thing to do with each plugin for my situation.

    Maverick's tip

    Want Claude to remember this between chats? Turn on memory in Claude's Settings, or save the summary into a Project you work in.

    2

    Try Each Plugin

    Type / in the message box (or click +) to see every skill your plugins added. You can also just ask in plain words, like the prompts below. Claude picks the right plugin on its own.

    Marketing: a full campaign in one go

    Prompt 2 · Copy & paste
    Plan a 30-day campaign for [YOUR PRODUCT OR OFFER] aimed at [WHO IT'S FOR]. Give me the goal, the channels, a content calendar, three ad ideas and a 4-email sequence, all in my brand voice.

    Finance: what changed in your numbers

    Attach a spreadsheet or export of your income and costs, then send:

    Prompt 3 · Copy & paste
    Build me an income statement from this file, compare it with last month and explain the biggest changes in plain English. Then tell me the three things I should look at first.

    Legal: review before you sign

    Attach the contract, then send:

    Prompt 4 · Copy & paste
    Review this contract for me. I'm the [CLIENT / FREELANCER / BUYER / SELLER]. Flag every risky clause in plain English, rate each one low, medium or high, and tell me exactly what to ask them to change.

    Heads up: Claude flags risks, it isn't your lawyer. For anything big, run its notes past one.

    Design: an honest critique

    Paste a screenshot of your site, slide or app, then send:

    Prompt 5 · Copy & paste
    Critique this design. Tell me what looks generic or AI-made, what's hard to read, and any accessibility problems. Then give me the five changes that would make the biggest difference, in order.

    Productivity: run your day

    Prompt 6 · Copy & paste
    /start

    Claude creates your task list, builds a memory of your people and projects, and opens a dashboard. Then connect your email and calendar in Customize > Connectors (Gmail and Google Calendar, or Microsoft 365) and send `/update` each morning to catch missed to-dos.

    Common Mistakes and Quick Fixes

    If this happensTry this
    No Plugins tabPlugins need a paid plan. On a work account, your admin may have turned them off.
    Can't find a pluginIn Customize > Plugins > Discover, search its exact name and look for by Anthropic.
    Claude doesn't use the pluginType / and pick the skill by name, or say "Use the Legal plugin for this."
    Answers sound genericSend the setup prompt first, and give real examples of your writing and work.
    Productivity can't see your email or calendarConnect them in Customize > Connectors, then send /update again.
    Finance numbers look offCheck the file has clear column names and dates, then ask Claude to list what it read before it calculates.
    Some plugin features are grayed outA few parts only run in Cowork or Claude Code. The skills and commands still work in chat.
