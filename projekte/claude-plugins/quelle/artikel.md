## Install All 5
Do this on a computer, at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) or in the Claude desktop app.
- Open Customize. Click Customize in the left sidebar.
- Open Plugins. Click the Plugins tab at the top, then Discover.
- Add each one. Search Marketing and click the + on the card made by Anthropic. Do the same for Finance, Legal, Design and Productivity.
- Check they're in. Click Yours. All five should be listed there.

Maverick's tip
Not sure a plugin is worth it? Click Try on the card to test it in a chat first.

Heads up: Search shows lots of plugins with similar names. Pick the ones that say by Anthropic under the name.

## The Setup Prompt
Open a new chat and send this. Claude asks you a few quick questions and sets all five plugins up around your work, so everything after this sounds like you.

Prompt 1 · Copy & pasteCopy

I just installed your Marketing, Finance, Legal, Design and Productivity plugins. Set them up for me.

Ask me one question at a time, wait for my answer, then ask the next. Find out:
1. What I do and who I do it for.
2. My brand voice, with an example of something I've written.
3. The numbers I track for money (revenue, costs, anything else).
4. The kinds of contracts I sign most.
5. What I'm designing or building right now.
6. My top 3 priorities this week.

When you're done, give me a short summary of what you learned, then tell me the single best first thing to do with each plugin for my situation.

## Try Each Plugin
Type / in the message box (or click +) to see every skill your plugins added. You can also just ask in plain words, like the prompts below. Claude picks the right plugin on its own.

### Marketing: a full campaign in one go

Prompt 2 · Copy & pasteCopy

Plan a 30-day campaign for [YOUR PRODUCT OR OFFER] aimed at [WHO IT'S FOR]. Give me the goal, the channels, a content calendar, three ad ideas and a 4-email sequence, all in my brand voice.

### Finance: what changed in your numbers
Attach a spreadsheet or export of your income and costs, then send:

Prompt 3 · Copy & pasteCopy

Build me an income statement from this file, compare it with last month and explain the biggest changes in plain English. Then tell me the three things I should look at first.

### Legal: review before you sign
Attach the contract, then send:

Prompt 4 · Copy & pasteCopy

Review this contract for me. I'm the [CLIENT / FREELANCER / BUYER / SELLER]. Flag every risky clause in plain English, rate each one low, medium or high, and tell me exactly what to ask them to change.

Heads up: Claude flags risks, it isn't your lawyer. For anything big, run its notes past one.

### Design: an honest critique
Paste a screenshot of your site, slide or app, then send:

Prompt 5 · Copy & pasteCopy

Critique this design. Tell me what looks generic or AI-made, what's hard to read, and any accessibility problems. Then give me the five changes that would make the biggest difference, in order.

### Productivity: run your day

Prompt 6 · Copy & pasteCopy

/start
Claude creates your task list, builds a memory of your people and projects, and opens a dashboard. Then [connect your email and calendar](/resources/complete-guide-to-claude-connectors-2026) in Customize > Connectors (Gmail and Google Calendar, or Microsoft 365) and send `/update` each morning to catch missed to-dos.

## Common Mistakes and Quick Fixes

 |
 | If this happens | Try this

 | No Plugins tab | Plugins need a paid plan. On a work account, your admin may have turned them off.

 | Can't find a plugin | In Customize > Plugins > Discover, search its exact name and look for by Anthropic.

 | Claude doesn't use the plugin | Type / and pick the skill by name, or say "Use the Legal plugin for this."

 | Answers sound generic | Send the setup prompt first, and give real examples of your writing and work.

 | Productivity can't see your email or calendar | Connect them in Customize > Connectors, then send /update again.

 | Finance numbers look off | Check the file has clear column names and dates, then ask Claude to list what it read before it calculates.

 | Some plugin features are grayed out | A few parts only run in Cowork or Claude Code. The skills and commands still work in chat.
